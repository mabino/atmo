# Atmo 1.7.0

Atmo 1.7 adds volume controls for the selected Apple TV.

## New

- **Volume Up / Volume Down** — new volume buttons in the remote (full and mini layouts), Remote menu items with Control-Command-= and Control-Command-Minus shortcuts, `atmo://volumeup` and `atmo://volumedown` URL commands, and matching Shortcuts actions. Volume presses behave like the Siri Remote's volume rocker, so they also control TVs and receivers over HDMI-CEC

## Notes

- Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later
